# Rate-limit coverage

Every `src/app/api/**/route.ts` is listed. Counts are **per identity per fixed window**; identities are `IP` (`clientIdentifier(request)`), `UID` (signed-in `user.userId`/`auth.uid`) or `EMAIL` (normalised registration address). Denied calls return `429` with `Retry-After`. Windows are seconds (`60` = 1 min, `300` = 5 min, `600` = 10 min, `3600` = 1 h, `86400` = 24 h).

Guard scopes expand as `organiser:<scope>`, `super-admin:<scope>` or `applications:<scope>`. A guard also enforces same-origin, role and the platform write gate before the counter is consumed.

## Residual notes

- The Firestore implementation is a fixed-window counter. It permits boundary bursts and is not a replacement for edge/WAF quotas (documented in `src/lib/rate-limit/index.ts`).
- `/api/profile/account` and `/api/webhooks/resend` are deliberately not user-rate-limited: the former requires a fresh session and a completed destructive operation is not retried, the latter authenticates the provider with an HMAC. `/api/health` is a constant response.
- The CSP, Turnstile and registration/login-attempt policies are listed for completeness but were not modified by this review; those files are owned by the concurrent hardening work.
